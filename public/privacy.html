Privacy notice

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Who handles your enquiry?

Newton Revenue enquiries are handled by Newton Strategic Holdings Ltd (UK company number 17330041). Contact us at newtonrevenue@gmail.com for privacy questions or a request to access or delete your information.

What we collect and why

When you submit our form, we receive your name, business, work email and the task you describe. We use it solely to assess and respond to your request, based on our legitimate interests in handling business enquiries. We do not use this form to subscribe you to a marketing list. Do not submit special-category or confidential third-party data.

Limited business-to-business outreach

Separately from the website enquiry form, Newton Revenue may research public information about UK companies to identify where our enquiry-handling and administrative services may be relevant. We may record a company's name and registration status, its public website and service descriptions, the source page, a publicly listed business email address and, if relevant, the name and professional role of a business representative. Sources can include a company's own website, the UK Companies House register and professional business directories. We do not buy indiscriminate bulk email lists.

We use these details to assess whether a carefully targeted, relevant one-to-one business message is appropriate, to manage replies, and to avoid repeated or unwanted contact. Where UK GDPR applies, our proposed lawful basis for limited B2B outreach is legitimate interests, subject to our documented purpose, necessity and balancing checks. PECR marketing rules vary by recipient: we do not treat sole traders or certain partnerships as corporate subscribers. A company being listed publicly is not, by itself, permission to market to every person in it.

If we contact you, our message will identify Newton Revenue and Newton Strategic Holdings Ltd, explain the connection to your business, and provide an easy way to object or opt out. You can object to direct marketing at any time by replying to our message or emailing newtonrevenue@gmail.com with “stop business marketing”. We will stop using your details for marketing and retain only minimal details on a suppression list where necessary to prevent further contact. We do not use marketing pixels in our outgoing one-to-one emails.

We periodically review research and outreach records for accuracy, relevance and continued necessity, removing material that is no longer required. Records needed for legal obligations, dispute handling or to honour marketing objections may be retained for those purposes. Providers of our business email and internal records may process relevant information on our behalf under their terms. We do not sell prospect or enquiry information to list brokers.

Storage, sharing and retention

The initial submission is stored in our configured Cloudflare infrastructure for up to 30 days. Only authorised staff should be able to retrieve it. We do not sell submitted contact details. Providers supporting our website and email may process the data according to their terms. If you become a customer, different contract and record-retention terms may apply and will be explained separately.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, erase or restrict personal data, or object to processing where the law provides that right. Contact newtonrevenue@gmail.com. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not intentionally set marketing cookies or load advertising trackers. Standard website-security and access logs may be processed by the hosting provider.
