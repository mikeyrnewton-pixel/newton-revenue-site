YOUR FIRST FIVE MINUTES

The reset you can finish before you overthink it.

01 · ONE MINUTE

Clear one surface

Desk, kitchen counter or the chair holding tomorrow's decisions. Stop after one spot.

02 · TWO MINUTES

Write one useful action

Make it physical and small. “Open the letter” beats “sort my life out”.

03 · TWO MINUTES

Do the first movement

Move the item, send the required note, or put trainers by the door. Finished is the win.

The rule: make tomorrow slightly easier; you don't need to rebuild everything by lunchtime.
