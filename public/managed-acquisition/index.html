WHAT WE COULD MANAGE

From interest to a properly handled sales opportunity.

01 — ATTRACT

Customer acquisition

Research target customers, prepare campaigns and landing pages, and manage approved advertising where the budget and account access are agreed. Inbound and partnerships come first where sensible.

02 — QUALIFY

Better enquiries

Capture voluntary enquiries, screen for location, service fit, urgency and intent, and flag missing information. We agree what counts as a qualified lead before a campaign begins.

03 — CONVERT

Follow-up and handoff

Organise replies, reminders and handovers so interested buyers reach the right person. Human approvals, consent checks and clear reporting stay central to the process.
