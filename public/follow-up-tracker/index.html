SET IT UP IN FIVE MINUTES

Keep it simple enough to use.

Open the CSV in your existing spreadsheet app. No new software and no complex automation necessary.

  1. Download and open. Add your current enquiries in your own private copy.
  2. Assign one owner. If nobody owns it, mark it for immediate review.
  3. Add one next action and due date. Avoid vague statuses like “in progress”.
  4. Review the open rows daily. Decide who needs a reply, a reminder or an escalation.

Keep customer records inside your organisation's authorised systems. Follow your own privacy and retention policies; this template is not a secure CRM or legal compliance solution.
